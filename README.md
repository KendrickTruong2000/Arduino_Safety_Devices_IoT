This was a Capstone Project at Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology (RMIT) University in Vietnam.
This Capstone Project was collaborated with Intel warehouse Vietnam. As our partner was looking for simple solution for managing safety in warehouse environment as workers and heavy machines operating in the same area.
The project was designed as follow:
- Using two ESP8266 placing on both end of a aisle.
- Both ESP8266 were connected to Wi-Fi.
- Each was electronicly connected to a button.
- As workers going to the aisle, they press the button, and the ESP8266 that detects button press will send signal to the other one at the other end, and wanring LED light up.
- The signal will be also sends to a dashboard on ThingsBoard.
